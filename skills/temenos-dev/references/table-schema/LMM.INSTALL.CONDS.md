Setting of fields ACC CYCLE L CCY and ACC CYCLE F CCY to "daily" overrides this field. If fields ACC CYCLE L CCY and ACC CYCLE F CCY are set to "monthly" frequency then, where interest on a contract is based on a floating rate (e.g. Base Rate) the user is allowed the alternative of instructing the system to calculate interest on a daily basis and pass accounting entries accordingly. Alternatively, the system may be instructed to merely inspect the interest rate on a daily basis but carry out an interest accrual only when there has actually been a rate change on a contract. The following example will clarify the significance of each of these alternatives:- Suppose that a contract runs thus:- Value Day Day Day Day Day Mat. Date 1 2 3 4 5 Date 10% 10% 10% 11% 11% 9% 9% A) Input of '1' at this field will instruct the system to carry out the interest accruals on a daily basis and pass accounting entries accordingly. This means that on day 1 the system will calculate interest at 10% on the contract for one day and pass the appropriate accounting entries. Similarly, at day 2 it will again calculate interest at 10% on the contract for one day and pass the accounting entries. At day 3 there is a rate change to 11% and the system will therefore calculate interest on the contract for one day at 11% and pass entries and so on through to maturity of the contract. B) Input of '2' at this field will instruct the system only to carry out interest accruals when there has been an actual rate change. Thus on each of days 1 and 2 the system will merely check the interest rate on the contract, find that the rate rate has remained at 10% throughout, and will not therefore carry out an interest accrual. However, at day 3 the system will check the interest rate, find that it has moved up from 10% to 11% and will then calculate interest on the contract for two days at 10% and pass the appropriate accounting entries. It will then continue to check the rate on a daily basis until it finds that at day 5 the rate fell back from 11% to 9% when it will then accrue interest on the contract for a further two days, but at 11% and again pass the appropriate accounting entries. It will continue with this procedure until contract maturity.
